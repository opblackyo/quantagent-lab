# Project license decision

On 2026-10-24 the owner explicitly selected MIT with attribution to **QuantAgent Lab contributors**. The exact grant is in root LICENSE. This selection does not authorize GitHub creation, push, release or public deployment.

The source distribution retains shadcn's MIT notice and TradingView chart attribution. TradingAgents and AI Hedge Fund were design references, not copied implementations; examined revisions are in REUSE_AUDIT.md. Dependency licenses remain separate and are inventoried in THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md and DEPENDENCY_LICENSES.json.

The candidate includes source, lockfiles and synthetic examples. It does not distribute installed environments, native binaries, real market data or provider responses. A future binary/container distribution requires its own dependency notice/source-availability review.
