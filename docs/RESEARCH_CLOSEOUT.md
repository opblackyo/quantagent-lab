# Research software closeout

This snapshot closes the local research/education alpha. It is a normal descendant of the already-public repository, with reviewed application/test source, locked dependencies, MIT attribution and synthetic examples. It does not deploy a website or provide broker/exchange orders. No new feature or paid-research milestone is opened.

## What the evidence supports

The local Mock/Replay workflow lets readers inspect immutable data hashes, past-only features, single-agent or fixed Analysis–Critic–Decision roles, schema validation, deterministic risk, next-valid-open simulation and ledgers. Mock results are fixed-rule demonstrations, not live LLM evidence.

Preliminary profitability is **PROFITABILITY_NOT_ESTABLISHED**. An available short historical example has positive price returns, but does not establish a reliable incremental risk-adjusted benefit or future earnings. Its single/multi-agent decisions produced the same targets and portfolio path, while using different inference work. Different realized exposure, small samples, model training exposure, transaction costs and inference costs prevent a causal architecture or income claim. Product revenue also lacks customer/payment validation in the available evidence. Not established does not mean profitability is impossible. Private source data, responses, reports and billing records are not redistributed.

## Live acceptance exception

The final closeout attempted a bounded read-only tariff/settlement reconciliation. Applicable native-unit conversion and query/failure fee evidence remained unresolved, so its actual UI acceptance was **blocked before inference**. No result from an old private pilot or an offline fixture is substituted for that acceptance. A connectivity call on synthetic prices would not settle profitability even if successful.

The experimental operator entry exists in the shipped source. Preview/startup/export/Replay do not load its scoped credential. Approved dispatch uses a server-held frozen scope and lazy key loader; the default app remains offline. Real HTTP and URL-mounted HTTP cannot use synthetic settlement, and a live profile cannot reach fixture reconciliation by sharing a fixture name. Missing verified charge evidence keeps the full hold and pauses before risk/fill. There is no claim of a completed real billing-import integration.

A 45-second operator deadline limits local transport waits and bytes, with DNS-before-connect and whole-second socket-resolution residuals. It cannot certify upstream cancellation or absence of charges. Failed/uncertain sends must not be retried blindly. Aggregated usage evidence is not a per-call invoice.

## Interpretation and verification

Returns are price-only, excluding dividends and cash interest; there is no forced final liquidation. Historical model knowledge cannot be excluded by hiding dates. Short-period annualized metrics are not an earnings forecast or statistical significance test. Model costs remain separate from simulated equity.

Use the commands in [the operator guide](OPERATOR_GUIDE.md). Actual local checks and immutable publication identity/files are bound by the matching release receipt; hosted checks must finish in [Actions](https://github.com/opblackyo/quantagent-lab/actions/workflows/ci.yml). Old verification documents retain their original dates and counts and are not used as evidence for this updated snapshot. Source/privacy scans cover the exact shipped files and publication history, not arbitrary installed dependency caches.

MIT applies to this project's source with attribution to QuantAgent Lab contributors. Third-party notices remain applicable. No installed environments, private databases, real market prices, real API responses, local credentials or private operator evidence are included.
